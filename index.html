Average Level Difference Investment Rate Status

Investment-rate rule: above the 1-year average → 7.5%; at or below the 1-year average → 10%; at or below the 5-year average → 15%; at or below the 10-year average → 20%; then +5 percentage points for every additional five-year threshold through the 50-year average. Long-term tiers are shown for manual review; only the normal 7.5% / 10% amount is automatic.

Wilshire 5000 ÷ denominator

Raw relative-value ratios. Nothing is rebased to 100: each reading is simply the Wilshire 5000 proxy level divided by the denominator's dollar/unit value.

Loading hard-value ratios and valuation context…
Example: Wilshire ÷ gold means the Wilshire index level divided by dollars per ounce of gold. A rising ratio means stocks are rising relative to gold; a falling ratio means gold is rising relative to stocks. The same logic applies to minimum wage, silver, and oil.
Hard-value basket: minimum wage, gold, silver, and oil each carry a 25% weight. Because those four raw ratios use different units, the basket alone is indexed to 100 in April 1985. The four individual series remain raw Wilshire ÷ denominator ratios.
