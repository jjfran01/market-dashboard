Average Level Difference Investment Rate Status

Investment-rate rule: above the 1-year average → 7.5%; at or below the 1-year average → 10%; at or below the 5-year average → 15%; at or below the 10-year average → 20%; then +5 percentage points for every additional five-year threshold through the 50-year average. Long-term tiers are shown for manual review; only the normal 7.5% / 10% amount is automatic.
